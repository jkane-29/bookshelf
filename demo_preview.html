🎨 What You're Seeing

This is a preview of your 3D bookshelf! Try clicking on the book spines above to see the interactive features.

📐 Dynamic Sizing

Book heights and thicknesses are calculated from cover dimensions and page counts.

🎨 Color Extraction

Spine colors are automatically sampled from the actual cover images.

🔄 3D Animation

Smooth CSS3 transforms create realistic book movements.

📚 Interactive

Click once for cover, twice for back, three times to return to shelf.

🚀 Running the Full App

To see your complete bookshelf with all features:

  1. Make sure dependencies are installed: bundle install
  2. Run the server: ruby app_custom.rb books.json
  3. Open your browser to: http://localhost:4567

✨ Customize Your Shelf

  • Edit books.json to add your own books
  • Add cover images to the covers/ folder
  • Download covers from online sources or use your own
  • The app will automatically extract colors and calculate dimensions!
